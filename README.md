# Bank-Customer-Churn

## Objective
To identify the demographic attributes and customer behavioral that drive the levels of churn to improve customer satisfaction and customer retention

## Guiding Questions
- What is the overall churn rate within the customer base?
- What differences are there in churn rates across different demographic groups (gender, age, and estimated salary)?
- How do customer account behaviors, such as tenure, account balance, and the number of products held, relate to their likelihood of churning?
- How do credit card behaviors, including having a credit card, credit score, card type, and credit points earned, relate to the likelihood of customer churn?
- What is the relationship between customer complaints, satisfaction scores, and churn levels?

## Insights and Recommendations
1. Correlation between balance and churn.
    - Develop targeted strategies for customers with very low balances, including tiered interest rate bonuses, fee waivers, and rewards programs. Incentivize customers with high balances, including  wealth management services, priority banking services, and premium lending products.
2. Complaints are strong predictors of churn.
    - Prioritize and improve the complaint resolution process, such as establishing a centralized system for tracking complaints, creating a team dedicated to handle customer complaints, and automating follow-up mechanisms.
3. Lower churn among customers with more products.
    - Implement strategies to encourage customers to adopt more products, including bundling offers, highlighting the benefits of additional services, and providing personalized recommendations.
